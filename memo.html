Cardinal & Gold Core+ Fund

Industrial Property Investment Analysis - Southern California

March 2024 Team of 4 13.2% LP IRR $69.3M Acquisition

Executive Summary

Investment Thesis: The Cardinal & Gold Core+ Fund seeks to expand its Southern California industrial portfolio through strategic acquisition of institutional-quality assets. Our comprehensive analysis evaluated two distinct opportunities: a stabilized Amazon-tenanted asset in Redlands and a value-add redevelopment opportunity in Torrance.

Recommendation: We recommend proceeding with the acquisition of 2255 W. Lugonia Avenue in Redlands for $69.33M. This stabilized industrial asset with Amazon as the credit tenant delivers a 13.2% LP IRR with significantly lower execution risk, aligning perfectly with our Core+ investment mandate.

Key Drivers: Triple-net lease structure, below-market rents offering upside potential, strategic Inland Empire location with excellent logistics connectivity, and modern facility specifications meeting current tenant requirements.
